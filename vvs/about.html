Our story

Started in a vault.
Built on Spring St.

Vintage Vault Supply started with a simple rule: if we make it, it has to be worth keeping. We pull real vintage, buy the heaviest blanks we can find, and treat every piece by hand in our studio in the Historic Core: dyed, bleached, painted, cut and rebuilt.

The name says it. A vault is where you keep things that matter, and VVS is the grade jewelers give a stone that's nearly flawless. Everything we sell is graded the same way, so you know exactly what you're holding.
